Best Italian Restaurants Near Flower Mound

8 restaurants across Flower Mound, Highland Village, Argyle, Bartonville, Lantana & Lewisville

Italian food lovers are well served in the Flower Mound area. Mio Nonno Trattoria and Fiori offer elevated Italian dining in Flower Mound proper, while Bistecca in Highland Village is a destination-worthy steakhouse with Italian roots. For casual Italian, Alforno's and Rocco's are local favorites.
